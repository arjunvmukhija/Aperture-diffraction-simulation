Airy Disk Diameter & Diffraction Blur

The Airy disk is the bright central spot formed when a lens focuses light from a point. Its diameter indicates diffraction blur: a larger disk spreads light over a wider area and softens fine detail; a smaller disk means less diffraction blur.

\(d = 2.44\,\lambda\,\frac{f}{D}\)

λ = wavelength of light · f = focal length · D = aperture diameter
